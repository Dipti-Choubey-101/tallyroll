TALLYROLL - WINDOWS GUIDE (for complete beginners)
===================================================

What this is
------------
Tallyroll reads photos of restaurant bills (vegetable market, butcher, dairy,
gas, electricity...), sorts each one into a category, and gives you one
spreadsheet. The reading is done by Gemma, a free AI model that runs ON YOUR
OWN COMPUTER. Your bills never go to the internet.

You only need the internet ONCE, to download two free programs and the model.
After that it works offline.

What you need
-------------
 * A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer
 * 8 GB of memory (RAM) at least. 16 GB is better.
 * About 10 GB of free disk space
 * Internet for the first-time setup (about 3 GB of downloads)

You do NOT need to know any coding. You will only double-click things.


PART 1 - ONE-TIME SETUP (about 20 minutes, mostly waiting)
===========================================================

STEP 1. Unzip the project
-------------------------
 1. Find the file  tallyroll.zip  (probably in your Downloads folder).
 2. Right-click it, then click "Extract All...", then click "Extract".
 3. A normal folder called  tallyroll  opens. Keep it somewhere easy, for
    example move it to your Desktop.
 IMPORTANT: do not run anything from inside the zip. Extract first.

STEP 2. Install Python (a free helper program)
----------------------------------------------
 1. Open your browser and go to   https://www.python.org/downloads/
 2. Click the big yellow "Download Python" button.
 3. Open the downloaded file to start the installer.
 4. On the FIRST screen, at the bottom, tick the box
        [x] Add python.exe to PATH
    This is the one step people miss. Then click "Install Now".
 5. When it says "Setup was successful", click Close.

STEP 3. Install Ollama (this runs the AI on your computer)
----------------------------------------------------------
 1. Go to   https://ollama.com/download   and click "Download for Windows".
 2. Run the downloaded  OllamaSetup.exe  and click Install.
 3. When it finishes, Ollama starts by itself. Look at the bottom-right of
    your screen (the little arrow near the clock). You should see a llama icon.
    That means it is running. You never need to open it again; it stays in
    the background.

STEP 4. Download the Gemma model (one time, about 3 GB)
-------------------------------------------------------
 1. In the tallyroll folder, double-click   setup-model.bat
 2. If Windows shows a blue box "Windows protected your PC":
       click "More info", then click "Run anyway".
    (It shows this for any file that did not come from the Microsoft Store.
     The file is plain text. You can right-click it and choose Edit to read it.)
 3. A black window opens. Type 1 and press Enter.
        1 = gemma3:4b   small, quick, best for most laptops
        2 = gemma4:e4b  newer, reads messy bills better, bigger download
 4. Wait until it says "Done". The download can take 10 to 30 minutes.
    Leave the window open. If your internet drops, just run it again.
 5. Press any key to close the window.


PART 2 - USING TALLYROLL (every time)
=====================================

STEP 5. Start it
----------------
 1. Double-click   start.bat   in the tallyroll folder.
 2. A black window opens and your web browser opens Tallyroll
    (address:  http://127.0.0.1:8765 ).
 3. KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you use Tallyroll. It is the engine.
    To stop Tallyroll, just close the black window.
 4. The pill at the top right should say "Gemma ready, on this computer"
    with a green dot. If it does not, see Troubleshooting below.

STEP 6. Try the sample bills first
----------------------------------
 1. Click "Try the sample bills". Eight made-up bills are read one by one.
 2. Watch the printer: each bill is scanned, then printed out as a receipt
    with a stamp showing its category.
 3. NOTE: the first bill is the slowest, because the model has to wake up.
    On a laptop WITHOUT a graphics card, expect roughly 20 seconds to 2 minutes
    per bill. That is normal. A computer with a good graphics card is faster.
 4. Do not worry about mistakes. Click any bill to correct it.

STEP 7. Use your real bills
---------------------------
 1. Put photos of the bills on the computer (send them from your phone to
    yourself by WhatsApp Web, email, or a USB cable).
 2. Drag the photos onto the printer, or click "Choose bill photos".
 3. Tips for good results:
      - One bill per photo.
      - Flat surface, good light, no shadow across the paper.
      - Fill the photo with the bill. Do not leave a big empty table around it.
      - Phone photos are fine. Screenshots are fine.
      - PDFs are not supported yet: take a screenshot of the PDF instead.
 4. Bills marked "Check" in red have something missing or odd, for example the
    date could not be read, or the items do not add up to the total.
    Click the bill, look at the photo next to the numbers, fix it, press Save.

STEP 8. Give the spreadsheet to your accountant
-----------------------------------------------
 1. Choose the month at the top of "The books" section.
 2. Click "Download spreadsheet". You get a .csv file that opens in Excel.
 3. If a month has bills marked "Check", fix them first. The file also has a
    column "Needs a check" so nothing is hidden.

Where is my data?
-----------------
 In the folder  tallyroll\data  . Your bill photos and the list of bills are
 there. Nothing is sent anywhere. To start fresh: Settings (gear icon) then
 "Delete all bills and photos".


DEMO MODE (for a backup, or for recording a video)
==================================================
Gear icon, then switch on "Demo mode". Tallyroll then recognises only the
eight sample bills and needs no model. Every bill added this way is tagged
"demo" so nobody is misled. TURN IT OFF before showing real work.


TROUBLESHOOTING
===============

"Python is not installed yet" (black window)
   Install Python (Step 2). Make sure you tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
   If you already installed it without ticking, run the installer again,
   choose "Modify", and tick the PATH option; or uninstall and reinstall.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Pill says "Ollama is not running"
   Press the Windows key, type Ollama, press Enter. Wait 10 seconds. If it is
   not installed, do Step 3.

Pill says "Gemma is not installed yet"
   Run setup-model.bat again (Step 4). Or open Settings and make sure the
   model name matches the one you downloaded (gemma3:4b or gemma4:e4b).

Reading is very slow, or the computer freezes
   Close other big programs (browser with many tabs, games). Use gemma3:4b,
   not the bigger model. Process fewer bills at once. Computers with 8 GB of
   memory can be slow; that is expected.

"The model answered, but not in a form Tallyroll could read"
   Just add that bill again. If it keeps happening, the photo may be too blurry.

Numbers look wrong
   Small AI models can misread digits, especially on blurry or crumpled bills.
   That is why Tallyroll shows the photo next to the numbers and marks doubtful
   bills. Always glance at the total of important bills.

The page says "Tallyroll could not start"
   The black window was closed. Double-click start.bat again.

"Address already in use" / port problem
   Tallyroll tries ports 8765 to 8775 automatically. Read the address shown in
   the black window and open that one.

Excel shows strange symbols instead of the rupee sign
   Open Excel first, then Data > From Text/CSV and choose "UTF-8".
   (Normally it just works.)

Nothing works at all
   Restart the computer, open Ollama from the Start menu, double-click start.bat.


RECORDING YOUR DEMO VIDEO (about 90 seconds)
============================================
 Windows has a free recorder: press Windows key + Alt + R to start and stop.
 The video is saved in  Videos\Captures .
 Suggested order:
   0:00  Say the problem in one sentence: "Restaurants drown in paper bills at
         tax time."
   0:10  Show the page. Turn OFF Wi-Fi on camera. (Proves it is offline.)
   0:20  Drop 3 or 4 real bills on the printer. Let the printing animation play.
   0:50  Click one flagged bill, show the photo, fix a number, Save.
   1:10  Click "Download spreadsheet" and open it in Excel.
   1:25  One sentence on why it runs on your own computer.
 Do not use demo mode in the video unless you say so out loud.


SHOWING IT TO THE RESTAURANT OWNER
==================================
 This is worth bonus points, so do it properly and honestly:
   1. Ask for 10 real bills from the last month (supplier bills, gas, power).
   2. Run them with the owner watching. Note: how many it read right, how many
      it flagged, how many it got wrong, and the time it took.
   3. Ask: "Would this save you time?" "What would you want next?" and
      "Would you pay for it? How much, per month?"
   4. Write down their answers in their own words. Ask permission before you
      mention their name or restaurant in your post.
   5. Never invent a quote or a result. If they said "interesting but not
      sure", write exactly that. Honest results are better than perfect ones.
 Put the real numbers into POST-DRAFT.md where you see [SQUARE BRACKETS].


PUTTING THE CODE ON GITHUB (needed for the Code section)
========================================================
 1. Create a free account at https://github.com
 2. Click + (top right) > New repository. Name it  tallyroll , keep it Public,
    click Create repository.
 3. Click "uploading an existing file". Open your tallyroll folder, select
    everything EXCEPT the  data  folder (it holds your private bills!), drag it
    into the browser, wait, then click "Commit changes".
    Hidden folder note: the  .github  folder holds the automatic test; if it
    does not upload by dragging, use Add file > Create new file and name it
    .github/workflows/test.yml , then paste the contents from your copy.
 4. Paste your repository link into the "Code" section of your DEV post.
 NEVER upload the  data  folder. It contains real bills.
