Tallyroll reads photos of supplier bills and receipts, sorts each one into a kitchen category, and gives you a single spreadsheet. The reading is done by Gemma, an open model running on this computer, so your bills never touch the internet.

  • Works with no internet connection
  • No per-bill fee, no account, no subscription
  • Phone photos, JPG, PNG or WEBP
